### On the workbench: Fez

A small decision model and a Bittensor training competition. Miners train candidates; validators measure how well they perform.

Local training and evaluation are working. Fez is experimental: its first live testnet training round and a published model release are still ahead.

[Explore the code](https://github.com/ooo-hq/fez) · [See the benchmarks](https://fez.chat/model) · [Read the experiments](https://github.com/ooo-hq/fez/blob/main/docs/experiments.md)
